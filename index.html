FAQs

Common questions
How do I start a test?

Open Tests, pick any chapter, subject, smart or mock test, and tap Start Test.

What is Smart Test Mode?

Smart Test mixes questions from every subject in one timed test, like the real MDCAT pattern.

Is there negative marking?

No. A wrong or unattempted answer scores 0, and every correct answer scores 1 mark.

Where can I see my results?

Every finished test appears on the Dashboard with your score, accuracy and a full answer review.
