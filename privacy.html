Privacy Policy — MDCAT Prime

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. Who we are

MDCAT Prime is a free MDCAT preparation app for students in Pakistan, developed by Muhammad Aslam Samejo. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

2. Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your full name and father's name
  • Your email address
  • Your password, which is handled securely by our authentication provider (see section 4)

When you use the app, we also store:

  • Your test attempts, answers and scores
  • Your reading progress in PDF notes
  • Your flashcard and study progress

Your profile photo, if you set one, stays on your own device and is not uploaded to our servers.

3. How we use your information

We use your information only to run the app: to sign you in, to save and show your test results and progress, and to provide the study material you open. We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not show advertisements inside the app.

4. Where your data is stored

Your account and study data are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore), the secure cloud service the app uses. Only the app's administrator can view student records, for support and for managing accounts (for example, suspending an account that breaks the rules).

5. Your choices

You can update your name in the app's Profile section. If you want your account and its data deleted, email us at mdcatprime@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, and we will delete it.

6. Children

MDCAT Prime is made for MDCAT aspirants, who are generally aged 17 and above. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
